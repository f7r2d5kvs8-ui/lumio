Lumio · For families

Privacy policy

Effective date: 16 August 2026

Who Lumio is for

Lumio is a learning app for children. An adult must manage an online account. Children can also play as a guest.

Information we process

  • Parent account: an adult’s email address, Supabase user identifier, login/session information, and, if chosen, Google sign-in information.
  • Cloud learning progress: level identifiers, current activity position, completion status, and update time linked to the parent-managed account.
  • Privacy-safe usage analytics: a random on-device identifier, session identifier, account identifier when signed in, app version, platform, selected language, and meaningful lesson/game start, completion, and progress events. Event metadata is restricted to a short allowlist and cannot contain names or free text.
  • On-device profile: the child’s first name, names written in another script, preferences, stars, streaks, and a local copy of progress. These values stay in the browser or app storage and are not uploaded by the current Lumio release.
  • Technical information: hosting, authentication, and content-delivery providers may receive ordinary connection data such as IP address, browser type, and request time when the service is used.

Why we use it

We use account information to authenticate the adult, learning progress to restore lessons across devices, local profile information to personalise writing practice, and limited aggregate analytics to understand discovery, use, retention, language demand, and activity completion. We do not record every tap, use analytics for advertising, build advertising profiles, or sell personal data. Lumio contains no third-party advertising.

Adult consent and choices

Account holders confirm that they are at least 18. Lumio does not collect a date of birth. Guest mode remains available, and an adult can delete an account at any time.

Service providers

Supabase provides authentication and cloud database services. Google processes sign-in information only when the adult chooses Google sign-in. The hosting and content-delivery services deliver Lumio’s files. These providers process data to supply their services; Lumio does not sell or share data for advertising.

Security

Network traffic uses HTTPS. Supabase stores authentication credentials and does not expose account passwords to Lumio. Access to cloud progress is restricted to the signed-in account. Raw analytics events cannot be read by normal or guest users; only an authorized administrator can request aggregate analytics. No system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Retention and deletion

Local profile data remains until it is cleared in the app/browser or the app is uninstalled. Active account and progress data remain until the adult deletes the account. The deletion action removes the active Supabase account and linked progress promptly. Limited residual copies may remain in secured provider backups or security logs for up to 30 days, after which they are deleted or de-identified unless a longer period is required by law, fraud prevention, or security obligations.

Your rights

Depending on location, a parent may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a copy of personal data and may withdraw consent. The fastest deletion route is the web account-deletion page; the same option appears in Lumio’s parent area.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be posted here with a new effective date. For a privacy question that is not an account-deletion request, contact the Lumio developer through the Lumio support tracker. Do not include a child’s name, email address, password, or other personal information in a public support request.
